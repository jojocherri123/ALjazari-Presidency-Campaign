Campaign Program

"Making school memorable and meaningful"

Our Vision

To create a fun yet beneficial school environment that may bring opportunities for students in the future.

Key Initiatives

1Islamic Events

Such as the qiyam that was done last year where we spend a fun yet beneficial time together in school. This benefits the students by making them knowledgeable in their religion in a fun way.

2Student Competitions

Such as a science fair, art competitions, mathematics related competitions. This benefits the students by giving them required skills and experience for their future.

3Social Events

Social events such as merchants day, talent show, and culture day. Such events benefit the students by teaching them business skills and various other skills.
