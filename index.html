Core Election Initiatives

1 Islamic & Cultural Events

Pillars: Character & Community

Goal: Reinforce our shared values through memorable, well-organized spiritual and cultural experiences. Building on the success of past events like the annual Qiyam night, we will create structured yet engaging gatherings where students bond, gain religious knowledge, and build strong character in a fun, supportive setting.

Concrete Promises:

  • Organize two annual overnight Qiyam & Youth Reflection Nights with structured group activities and discussion circles.
  • Host interactive faith-based trivia competitions with student recognition and awards.
  • Ensure all religious events are planned transparently without interrupting academic preparation.

2 Student Competitions & Academic Showcases

Pillars: Knowledge & Technology

Goal: Elevate student talent through competitive academic and creative platforms. By hosting structured events in science, mathematics, and the arts, we will help students build real-world skills, strengthen their college portfolios, and earn recognition for their unique abilities.

Concrete Promises:

  • Relaunch the Annual STEM & Innovation Fair, featuring student-led tech projects and robotics.
  • Establish an inter-grade Mathematics & Logic Challenge with peer mentoring.
  • Create an Arts & Digital Design Exhibition showcasing student visual media and creative writing.

3 Entrepreneurship & Social Events

Pillars: Community & Life Skills

Goal: Create dynamic social platforms that teach real-world practical skills while bringing energy to campus life. Events like Merchants Day, student talent showcases, and cultural exchange days will give students hands-on business experience, financial literacy, and memorable collaborative moments.

Concrete Promises:

  • Host an expanded Student Merchants Day where student entrepreneurs and clubs design, market, and operate real stalls.
  • Organize an annual Campus Talent & Culture Day celebrating diverse backgrounds and artistic skills.
  • Establish a transparent budgeting workshop so student stall leaders learn practical profit/cost management.

Our Commitment to Feasibility

Every promise in this platform is vetted for realistic scheduling, budget feasibility, and maximum student participation. We focus on quality execution rather than unachievable promises.
